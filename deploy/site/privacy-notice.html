Parking Tracker: privacy notice

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who is responsible

Parking Tracker is a personal project by Gihed Annabi, Belgium. Questions or requests: contact@pivotsoftwares.com.

What stays on your phone

Your parking card (number, plate, municipality, areas, dates), your Home and Work places, your parked car's position and your timer are stored only on your phone, in an encrypted database. They are never sent to our server and never synchronised. Card photos are read on the phone and are not kept or sent anywhere. Your position is used on the phone to check where you may park and to send parking alerts.

Signing in with Google

You sign in with your Google account. Google sends the app a one-time sign-in proof. Our server checks it and then forgets it. From your Google account we keep only a scrambled identifier (a keyed hash of your Google account ID). We don't keep your name, e-mail address or picture.

What our server stores

  • The scrambled account identifier.
  • The day your account was created and the day it was last used.
  • Your sign-in sessions, with hashed refresh tokens only.

We use this to keep you signed in (performance of the contract) and to protect the service against abuse (legitimate interest).

Map areas and searches

To answer "can I park here?", the app asks for parking data for the map area around you:

  • from our server, which uses the area to answer and does not store it, and never logs it next to your account; or
  • directly from the public sources: parking.brussels, Brussels Mobility, UrbIS and the City of Brussels. Address searches go to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service.

Those public services receive the map area or search text and your phone's network address, under their own privacy policies.

Where the data is kept

Our server runs at OVHcloud in the European Union. Server logs are kept 7 to 14 days and never contain e-mail addresses, tokens or precise positions.

How long

  • Until you delete your account, or after 24 months without any use.
  • Sessions last at most 90 days.
  • Server backups may keep a deleted account for up to 6 months. If a backup is ever restored, deleted accounts are removed again.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete my account). This deletes your account on our server and wipes the app's data on your phone.
  • Sign out keeps your data on the phone.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account at any time: https://myaccount.google.com/connections
  • You have the right to access, correct, erase or object to the processing of your data. Write to contact@pivotsoftwares.com. You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority: https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be

Changes

We will update this page when the app's use of data changes and show the date above.
